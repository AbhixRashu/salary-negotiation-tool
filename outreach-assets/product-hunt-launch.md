# Product Hunt Launch — SalaryPitcher.com

## Tagline

Write a counter-offer email in 2 minutes. Free, no login, backed by market data.

## Description

**Paragraph 1:** SalaryPitcher is a free AI-powered salary negotiation email generator that helps US professionals write data-backed counter-offer emails in under two minutes. No login, no credit card, no templates — just answer a few questions about your offer and get a personalized email you can copy, paste, and send.

**Paragraph 2:** Unlike generic negotiation advice blogs, SalaryPitcher actually generates the email for you. It factors in your role, location, experience level, industry, company size, and current offer — then produces a polished counter-offer email with market data built into the justification. You can choose your tone (collaborative, assertive, or data-driven) and polish the result in real-time.

**Paragraph 3:** Beyond the core generator, SalaryPitcher includes a salary calculator (federal + state tax breakdown), an offer comparison tool (up to 5 offers side-by-side with COL adjustment), and a growing library of negotiation guides. It's built for professionals who know they should negotiate but don't know exactly what to say.
